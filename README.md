## 1. Acquisition Overview

Golden Kin Atelier is a luxury fashion and beauty brand identity developed around heritage, craftsmanship, exclusivity, and contemporary presentation.

The visual direction uses a gold-and-black palette, an ornamental crest, a crowned dragon emblem, and the GK monogram. The brand presentation includes applications for fashion, beauty and cosmetics, premium collections, packaging, stationery, digital presence, and retail environments.

The brand is being presented for **full transfer of the specifically identified and transferable brand rights and digital assets**, subject to a written asset schedule and a mutually executed transfer agreement.

This repository is intended to support buyer review. It is not, by itself, a trademark certificate, legal title document, or completed transfer instrument.

## 2. Brand Identity

The identity system is built around these elements:

- **Primary name:** GOLDEN KIN ATELIER
- **Monogram:** GK
- **Emblem:** crowned dragon within an ornamental crest
- **Visual direction:** black, gold, and complementary metallic tones
- **Brand domains:** luxury fashion, beauty and cosmetics, atelier services, and premium lifestyle applications

Presentation images and mockups illustrate the intended brand applications. A mockup is not a substitute for the corresponding editable production file.

## 3. Digital Assets and Delivery Inventory

The deliverable set must be identified in a dated asset schedule before the transfer is completed. The schedule should identify each item by filename, format, version, and, where practical, SHA-256 checksum.

Potential delivery categories, **only where the corresponding files are present and transferable**, include:

- Logo and emblem source files
- Transparent and web-ready logo exports
- Brand color and typography specifications
- Packaging, stationery, and visual application files
- Brand presentation and guideline documents
- QR artwork and its destination URL
- Relevant domain or digital account transfer items, if owned and legally transferable
- Repository records and documentation specifically listed in the transfer schedule

No file format, account, domain, registration, certificate, or source asset should be considered included merely because it appears in a presentation image or is named in this README.

## 4. Ownership, Rights, and Transfer

The proposed transaction is a full transfer of the rights and assets that are:

1. Specifically identified in the final asset schedule;
2. Owned or lawfully controlled by the seller; and
3. Legally transferable under the applicable law.

Any trademark registration, application, jurisdiction, registration number, or other registered right must be verified against its underlying official record before it is represented as registered or included in the transaction.

The buyer may select the jurisdiction in which to pursue any further registration, recordal, or legal formalities, subject to applicable law and the requirements of the relevant authority. The transfer agreement must distinguish the rights transferred at closing from any later registration or recordal steps.

Following completion of the agreed transfer and delivery, the seller intends to retain only a non-operational archival copy for historical and evidentiary recordkeeping. That archival retention is not intended to confer continuing commercial, administrative, or operational control over the transferred brand or assets. The precise scope of archival retention and the effective transfer of rights must be stated in the signed agreement.

Evidence status must be stated precisely. **Presented does not mean approved; implemented does not mean verified; and a repository record does not replace legal title or a signed transfer agreement.**

## 7. Buyer Review and Transaction Sequence

A controlled acquisition process should follow this sequence:

1. Review the brand presentation and the actual available source assets.
2. Receive the dated asset schedule and ownership evidence relevant to each item.
3. Agree the purchase terms, exclusions, warranties, and transfer date.
4. Execute the written transfer agreement.
5. Deliver the listed files and transfer eligible accounts or rights using the relevant provider procedures.
6. Record delivery acknowledgements and preserve the agreed archival evidence.

Any legal, tax, trademark, or regulatory requirements should be reviewed by qualified advisers in the relevant jurisdiction.
